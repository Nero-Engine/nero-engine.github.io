Last updated 09-11-2026

This 3D viewer is made and hosted by Nero Engine Group Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, for the business whose name and logo appear on it. Nero Engine Group Ltd is responsible for the information described here.

What we record when you open the viewer

  • That the page was opened, which parts you tapped, whether you used the camera view, and roughly how long you stayed.
  • The kind of device (phone or computer) and browser, and the country and town your internet connection appears to come from. We do not keep your IP address.
  • If you opened a personal link from an email, the code in that link, so the sender knows their link was opened.

We use no cookies and no advertising or analytics companies. Fonts and code are served from our own address. The first time you open a viewer, your browser remembers on your own device that the short tour has played; nothing about that is sent to us.

If you send a question

If you use the "Ask" form, we receive your name, email, phone number if you give one, and your message. We use them only to answer you and pass them to the business named on the viewer so they can reply.

Why, and for how long

We record visits so the sender can see whether their viewer is useful (our legitimate interest), and we handle questions at your request. Visit records and questions are deleted after 13 months at most.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete what we hold about you, or object to it, by emailing hello@neroengine.com. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

About the model

The 3D model is an illustration made from public data. It is not to scale and is not a survey.
