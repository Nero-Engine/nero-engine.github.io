1 Streamside Drive: the facts

10 painted cabins

Furnished log-sided cabins built on site, each one painted its own color, with a covered front porch.

Source: REV Real Estate listing (Century 21 Commercial, MLS 10032823) and listing photos

5 covered wagons

Conestoga-style wagons that sleep 4 to 6 each, set on beds of river stone under the cottonwoods.

Source: REV Real Estate listing (Century 21 Commercial, MLS 10032823)

3,800 sq ft log building

Two stories. Bathrooms, laundry, the office and a communal area downstairs, the owner's residence upstairs with a big deck.

Room to grow (Concept · an idea, not a forecast)
