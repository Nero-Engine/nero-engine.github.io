Lake Trafford Marina: the facts

5.3 acres for $6,500,000

The store, the airboat docks, the RV sites and the canal to the lake, on one parcel at 6001 Lake Trafford Road, Immokalee.

Source: Coldwell Banker Preferred Properties sale sheet for Lake Trafford Marina, read 11-11-2026. Border drawn from the Florida statewide parcel map; illustrative, not a survey.

Airboats & Alligators

Airboat tours out on Lake Trafford, sunset cruises, a gift shop and a reptile zoo. The broker lists airboat tours, rentals, the retail store and RV site rentals.

Sources: airboatsandalligators.com, read 11-11-2026; Coldwell Banker Preferred Properties sale sheet for Lake Trafford Marina, read 11-11-2026 (CIMLS). Boats and people shown are illustrative.

Spot the alligators

The business is called Airboats & Alligators, and its reptile zoo lets visitors hold a baby one. A few have wandered into this model. One suns itself on the canal bank by the docks and slips into the water now and then. See how many you can find.

Source: airboatsandalligators.com, read 11-11-2026. The alligators shown are illustrative.

24 RV sites

Two rows of RV sites along the lane behind the store, each with its own hookup post.

Source: Coldwell Banker Preferred Properties sale sheet for Lake Trafford Marina, read 11-11-2026. Site positions from the USDA NAIP aerial photo; the rigs shown are illustrative.

1,138 ft of canal

A private canal runs from the docks straight out to Lake Trafford, so the tours leave from the door.

Source: Coldwell Banker Preferred Properties sale sheet for Lake Trafford Marina, read 11-11-2026. The line follows the canal on the USDA NAIP aerial photo.

About 1,500 acres of lake

Florida's largest lake south of Lake Okeechobee. Over 6 million cubic yards of muck were dredged from it in a public restoration that cost over $21 million and finished in 2010.

Sources: South Florida Water Management District, Lake Trafford Management Plan, October 2018; SFWMD news release, 1 March 2012

455,525 people within an hour

455,525 people live within a 1 hour drive, 2.06 million within 2 hours and 9.5 million within 3 hours. Collier County had 2,808,800 visitors in FY2024/25. The Seminole Casino Hotel is 12 minutes away, Ave Maria 25, Southwest Florida International Airport 38, Fort Myers 48 and Naples 65.

Sources: US Census 2020 with drive times (people); Valhalla routing on OpenStreetMap (drive times), read 11-11-2026; Naples, Marco Island, Everglades CVB FY2024/25 Tourism Impact Report (visitors)

Room to grow (Concept · an idea, not a forecast)

About 2.9 acres of the land are spare. The county's approved plan for the site allows up to 16 rental cabins, a waterfront restaurant, 23 boat slips and a nature education center. Shown as an idea only.

Sources: Coldwell Banker Preferred Properties sale sheet (about 2.9 acres spare); Collier County Ordinance 04-39, Lake Trafford Marina Mixed Use PUD (cabins, restaurant, slips, nature center). The layout is illustrative.
